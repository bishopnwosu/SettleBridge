Provider Questions

Here are some things you may want to know before applying.

Who can become a provider?

Businesses and independent professionals offering relevant local services may apply. Some services may require professional qualifications or licences.

Is registration automatically approved?

No. Applications are reviewed before providers and their services are published.

Does it cost money to apply?

There is no application fee during our initial provider onboarding phase.

How will customers contact me?

Initially, customers will be able to submit service enquiries through SettleBridge. Further contact and payment arrangements will be agreed directly with providers.
