Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information about SettleBridge.

What is SettleBridge?

SettleBridge is a marketplace that connects newcomers with local service providers.

Is SettleBridge available across Portugal?

We aim to serve customers throughout Portugal. Actual service availability depends on the providers registered in each location.

Are service providers verified?

Provider applications and relevant documents are reviewed before approved listings are published.

Does SettleBridge provide immigration or legal services?

No. Legal and immigration matters should be handled by appropriately qualified professionals.

Can I register my business?

Yes. Service providers can apply to join the SettleBridge network.

Does SettleBridge handle payments?

During the initial launch, customers arrange payment directly with service providers.
