Privacy Policy

We believe your personal information deserves respect and responsible handling. This policy explains how SettleBridge handles your information.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SettleBridge collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit our website, request a service, contact us or apply to become a service provider.

This policy is intended to apply to our marketplace activities in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

1. Who We Are

SettleBridge is a newcomer services marketplace that connects immigrants, international students, foreign workers and relocating families in Portugal with local service providers.

SettleBridge is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. This means we decide how and why it is used.

2. Information We Collect

A. When you request a service

When you use the "Request this service" form on a provider's page, we collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number, if you give it.
  • How you would like to be contacted.
  • Your preferred date, if you give one.
  • Your message describing what you need.
  • Which provider you contacted, and when.

Please only include the information needed to arrange the service. Do not include sensitive details, such as health information or identity document numbers, in your message.

B. When you apply to become a provider

When you apply through our "Become a Provider" form, we collect your name, email address, phone and WhatsApp numbers, business name and type, service category, country and city, service area, languages, pricing, service description, website, registration number, the type of supporting document you hold, and your confirmations and agreements.

C. When you contact us

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, the subject you choose and your message.

D. Administrator accounts

Only authorised SettleBridge administrators can log in to the website at present. For them, we process their email address and login session. Public user accounts are not yet available.

E. Technical information

When you visit our website, your device's IP address and basic browser information are received by the companies that host our website and deliver its fonts and code (see section 6). We do not use analytics or advertising tracking.

3. Public Provider Profiles

When we approve a provider application, we create a public profile on SettleBridge. Anyone visiting the website can see:

  • Business name.
  • Service category.
  • City, country and service area.
  • Service description.
  • Languages spoken.
  • Pricing type and starting price.

The provider's email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, registration number and document information are not shown publicly. Only SettleBridge administrators can see them.

If you are a sole trader, your business name may be your own name. You can ask us to change or remove your profile at any time.

4. Why We Use Your Information, and Our Legal Basis

  • Handling your service request, including contacting you and sharing your request with the provider you chose. Legal basis: your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the request form, and taking steps at your request before a service is arranged.
  • Reviewing provider applications and emailing you our decision. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement with you.
  • Publishing approved provider profiles. Legal basis: performing our agreement with the provider.
  • Answering messages sent through our contact form. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.
  • Keeping the website secure and preventing spam and misuse, for example limiting how many requests one email address can send in an hour. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the platform and its users.
  • Meeting legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or disclose information. Legal basis: legal obligation.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell it.

5. Who We Share Your Information With

The provider you contact

When you send a service request, we share your name, contact details, preferred date and message with the provider you chose, so they can tell us whether they can help or contact you about the service. Providers are independent businesses and are responsible for how they use your information after receiving it.

Technology companies that run our website

These companies process information on our behalf, only to provide their service to us (see section 6).

For legal reasons

We may disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the platform.

Where this happens, we rely on safeguards recognised under GDPR, such as an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the company takes part) or Standard Contractual Clauses included in the company's data processing terms.

8. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Service requests: up to 12 months after the request is closed, then deleted.
  • Rejected provider applications: up to 12 months after our decision, then deleted.
  • Approved providers: for as long as the provider is listed on SettleBridge, and up to 12 months after the listing ends.
  • Contact form messages: up to 12 months after we have answered.
  • Records we must keep by law, for example for tax or accounting: for the period the law requires.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see section 11).

9. Cookies and Browser Storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

When an administrator logs in, their browser stores a login session so they stay signed in. This is strictly necessary for the admin area to work, and it is not used for public visitors.

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

10. How We Protect Your Information

  • Our website uses a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Database security rules ensure that service requests, provider contact details and applications can only be read by authorised administrators. Visitors can send a request but cannot read anyone else's.
  • Provider contact details are kept separate from public profiles.
  • Secret keys, such as our email service key, are stored on the server and never in the website's public code.

No website can guarantee perfect security. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will inform you and the supervisory authority as the law requires.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Object to our use of your information based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive your information in a portable format, where this applies.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email nwosueloka2@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will reply within one month. Some rights have legal conditions and exceptions.

12. Children

SettleBridge is intended for adults. Requests involving children, such as family relocations, should be made by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

13. Third-Party Websites

Our website contains links to other websites, such as WhatsApp or providers' own websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their privacy policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The new version will be published on this page with an updated date. If a change significantly affects you, we will tell you directly where we can.
