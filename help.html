Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some answers to questions you may have about SettleBridge.

Finding Local Services

What is SettleBridge?

SettleBridge is a newcomer services marketplace designed to connect immigrants, international students, foreign workers and relocating families with local service providers who can help with everyday needs when settling into a new country.

What services can I find on SettleBridge?

Our planned service categories include airport transportation, home cleaning, moving assistance, translation and interpretation, and practical local assistance. Available services depend on the providers listed on the platform.

Which countries does SettleBridge currently serve?

Portugal is our initial launch market. Our long-term vision is to make SettleBridge useful to newcomers in other countries as the platform and provider network grow.

How do I find a service in my city?

Visit the Find Services page, select the type of assistance you need and enter your preferred city. You can explore the available providers and their service information.

Does SettleBridge provide visa or immigration services?

SettleBridge is a local services marketplace, not an immigration agency. We do not process visas or make immigration decisions. For legal or immigration matters, seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

Bookings and Payments

How do I request a service?

Find the service you need, review the provider's information and follow the contact or booking instructions provided on their listing.

How do I know how much a service costs?

Check the provider's listing for available pricing information. If the price is not clear, contact the provider to confirm the full cost before agreeing to a service.

How do payments work?

Payment arrangements depend on the provider and the service. Always confirm the payment method, amount and any additional charges directly with the provider before proceeding.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Cancellation and rescheduling arrangements may differ between providers. Review the provider's terms and contact them as early as possible if you need to make a change.

What should I do if I have a problem with a service?

First, contact the provider to explain the issue. If you need additional assistance, contact the SettleBridge support team through our Contact Us page.

For Service Providers

How can I become a SettleBridge provider?

Visit our Become a Provider page and complete the provider application form. Our team will review your submitted information and contact you about the next steps.

Who can register as a service provider?

Businesses and independent professionals offering relevant local services to newcomers may apply. Applicants must meet applicable registration, licensing and legal requirements for the services they offer.

Is there a fee to apply?

There is currently no application fee during our initial provider onboarding period. Any future provider charges will be communicated clearly before they apply.

When will my provider application be approved?

Each application will be reviewed before a provider is accepted onto the marketplace. We will contact applicants if additional information is required.

Can I update my service information?

Contact our support team to request changes to your provider or service information.

Accounts and General Support

Do I need an account to browse services?

You can explore the marketplace without creating an account. Some features may require registration as the platform develops.

How can I contact SettleBridge?

You can reach us by email or WhatsApp, or send a message through our Contact Us page.

How does SettleBridge handle my personal information?

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements. Please read our Privacy Policy for more information.
