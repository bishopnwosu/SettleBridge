Privacy Policy

We believe your personal information deserves respect and responsible handling. This policy explains how SettleBridge handles your information.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SettleBridge collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit our website, create an account, enquire about a service or apply to become a service provider.

This policy is intended to apply to our marketplace activities in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

1. Who We Are

SettleBridge is a newcomer services marketplace designed to connect immigrants, international students, foreign workers and relocating families with local service providers.

For data protection purposes, the organisation responsible for deciding how and why your personal information is processed is referred to as the data controller.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use SettleBridge, we may collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal and Contact Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Country or city of residence.
  • Information you provide when contacting us.

B. Account Information

If you create an account, we may process your account details, login credentials and account preferences. Authentication information should be handled through the relevant secure authentication service.

C. Service Provider Information

If you apply to become a provider, we may collect your business name, contact details, service categories, service locations, languages spoken, pricing information, business website and relevant verification information.

D. Enquiry and Booking Information

We may process information you provide when requesting a service, communicating with a provider, making an enquiry or arranging a booking.

E. Technical Information

Depending on the website services and technologies we use, technical information may include browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited and basic website usage information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Help users discover relevant local services.
  • Connect customers with service providers.
  • Review and manage provider applications.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Communicate important service updates.
  • Maintain and improve our website.
  • Protect the platform against fraud and misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Legal Grounds for Processing

Where GDPR applies, we will only process personal information when an appropriate legal basis exists. Depending on the situation, this may include:

  • Consent: Where you have freely agreed to a specific use of your personal information.
  • Contract: Where processing is necessary to provide a service or take steps requested before entering an agreement.
  • Legal obligation: Where processing is required to comply with applicable laws.
  • Legitimate interests: Where processing is necessary for legitimate business purposes and does not override your rights and freedoms.

5. How We Share Your Information

SettleBridge may share relevant information in the following circumstances:

With Service Providers

When you request or arrange a service, relevant contact and enquiry details may be shared with the provider so they can respond to your request.

With Technology and Service Partners

We may use third-party companies to provide website hosting, database services, authentication, communication, security and other technical functions. These companies should only process information under appropriate arrangements.

For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information where required by law, a valid legal request or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the platform.

We do not intend to sell your personal information to third parties.

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our technology providers may process information in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where personal information is transferred internationally and GDPR applies, we will use an appropriate transfer mechanism and safeguards where required by law.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, the reason it was collected, account status, service history and legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to any lawful retention requirements.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences and understand how visitors use the platform.

Where required, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies or using similar tracking technologies.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

9. How We Protect Your Information

We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration or disclosure.

No website, online service or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security. We will continue to review and improve our security arrangements as the platform develops.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Where GDPR applies, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information in applicable circumstances.
  • Request restrictions on certain processing activities.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Request a copy of your information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.
