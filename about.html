Who We Are

SettleBridge is a newcomer services marketplace designed to make settling into a new country easier.

We connect immigrants, international students, foreign workers and relocating families with local service providers who can help with everyday needs.

From airport transportation and cleaning to moving assistance and translation, our goal is to make useful local services easier to discover.

We are starting in Portugal with a vision to make SettleBridge useful to newcomers in other countries as our network grows.

Our Purpose

Everything we build is guided by a simple belief: settling somewhere new should be easier.

Our Mission

To connect people starting life in another country with reliable local service providers and practical assistance, helping them save time, reduce uncertainty and settle with confidence.

Our Vision

To become a trusted international marketplace where newcomers can discover useful local services and service providers can reach the people who need their expertise.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide how we serve newcomers and work with service providers.

Trust

We aim to make provider information and verification processes clear and transparent.

Accessibility

We want useful local services to be easier for people from different backgrounds to find.

Transparency

We believe clear service descriptions, pricing and expectations build confidence.

Convenience

We bring different practical services together in one accessible platform.

Community

We believe newcomers and local businesses can benefit from meaningful connections.

Continuous Improvement

We listen to feedback and improve our platform as the needs of our users evolve.

How We Want to Make a Difference

Finding reliable help in an unfamiliar country can take time and effort. People may not know where to search, who to trust or what a service should cost.

SettleBridge aims to simplify that process by bringing service information and local providers together in one place.

We also want to help local businesses reach new customers, creating opportunities for both newcomers and the communities welcoming them.

Let's Make Settling Easier Together.

Whether you're starting a new life in Portugal or offering services to help others settle, there's a place for you at SettleBridge.
